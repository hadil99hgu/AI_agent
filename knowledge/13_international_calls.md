# International Calls from Switzerland

International calls are calls made from Switzerland to a foreign number.
They are different from roaming, which applies when the customer's device is connected to a foreign network.

- Basic: international calls are not included.
- Plus: 200 minutes per month from Switzerland to Zone A countries.
- Premium: unlimited calls from Switzerland to Zone A countries for normal personal use.

Premium-rate numbers, satellite services and special service numbers are not included.
International calling allowances do not include mobile data roaming.
