# Data Add-ons

Basic customers who use the 20 GB Swiss monthly high-speed allowance can continue at reduced speed or purchase a high-speed add-on.

## Swiss High-Speed Add-on
- 10 GB
- CHF 9.90
- Valid until the end of the current billing cycle

Plus and Premium include unlimited Swiss data.
Roaming add-ons are separate products.
