# Cancellation and Suspension

Most standard mobile subscriptions can be cancelled with 60 days' notice to the end of a billing period.

A customer with an active minimum-term promotional commitment may have an early termination charge.
Any outstanding device-financing balance remains payable after cancellation.

## Temporary suspension
Customers may request temporary suspension for up to three months in a twelve-month period.
During suspension:
- normal mobile usage is unavailable;
- device financing continues;
- a reduced monthly suspension fee may apply.

Cancellation and suspension requests require customer authentication.
