# SIM and eSIM

An eSIM is a digital SIM profile installed on a compatible device.

To activate an eSIM:
1. Open the Alpine Mobile app or customer portal.
2. Request an eSIM for the selected mobile number.
3. Authenticate the account.
4. Scan the activation QR code or follow the device activation flow.
5. Confirm that the new eSIM has network connectivity.

Some devices support direct eSIM transfer to a new phone.
If direct transfer is unavailable, a new eSIM profile must be issued.

A physical SIM can be replaced if damaged, lost or incompatible with a new device.
