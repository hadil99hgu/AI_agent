# Changing a Mobile Subscription

## Upgrades
An upgrade to a higher-priced plan can take effect immediately.
The new monthly price is prorated for the remaining days of the current billing cycle.
New plan benefits become available once the upgrade is confirmed.

Allowed examples:
- Basic to Plus
- Basic to Premium
- Plus to Premium

## Downgrades
A downgrade normally takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
A downgrade may be restricted during a promotional minimum-term commitment.

Changing to the same plan has no effect and is not treated as an upgrade.

Account changes require customer authentication.
