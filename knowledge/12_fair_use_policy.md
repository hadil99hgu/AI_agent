# Fair Use Policy

Unlimited mobile plans are designed for normal personal mobile use.

Restricted examples may include:
- resale of network access;
- automated traffic that materially harms network stability;
- fraudulent or unlawful use;
- using a consumer SIM as large-scale permanent commercial connectivity.

Temporary traffic management may be applied to protect network integrity.
The fair-use policy does not change the roaming allowances defined in the roaming policy.
