# Roaming Policy

Roaming applies when an Alpine Mobile SIM or eSIM connects to a partner network outside Switzerland.

## Zone A
Zone A includes the European Union, the United Kingdom, Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein.

Included monthly roaming data:
- Basic: 2 GB
- Plus: 20 GB
- Premium: 40 GB

After the included allowance is used, roaming data is blocked by default unless the customer activates a roaming add-on.

## Zone B
Zone B includes the United States, Canada, Turkey, Australia, Japan and selected additional countries.

Only Premium includes a monthly Zone B allowance: 5 GB.
Basic and Plus customers need a Zone B roaming add-on before using mobile data in these destinations.

## Cost protection
Data roaming is disabled automatically when no included allowance or active add-on is available.
Customers can change roaming settings in the mobile app.
