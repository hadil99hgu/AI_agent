# Lost or Stolen Phone

1. Block the SIM or eSIM as quickly as possible.
2. Change important account passwords if compromise is suspected.
3. Use the manufacturer's remote-lock or location service when available.
4. Request a replacement SIM or eSIM.
5. Report theft to the police when appropriate.

Blocking the SIM does not erase data stored on the device.
The mobile number can normally be retained after SIM replacement.
