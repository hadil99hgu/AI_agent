# PIN, PUK and SIM Lock

A SIM PIN protects the SIM from unauthorized use.
After several incorrect PIN attempts, the SIM becomes locked and requires the PUK code.

The PUK code is available in the authenticated customer portal.
Repeated incorrect PUK attempts may permanently block the SIM.

If permanently blocked, the customer needs a replacement SIM or eSIM.
Support agents must never disclose a PUK code without identity verification.
