# Billing and Payments

Monthly invoices may contain subscription fees, device instalments, roaming add-ons, international calls, third-party purchases and one-time fees.

## First invoice
The first invoice may differ from the normal monthly price because the subscription fee can be prorated from activation to the end of the billing cycle.

## Payment methods
Supported methods:
- eBill
- credit or debit card
- bank transfer
- TWINT

If an invoice remains unpaid after reminders, outbound services may be restricted.

For live invoice amount, payment status or due date, use the billing API rather than this document.
