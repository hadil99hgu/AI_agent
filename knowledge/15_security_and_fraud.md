# Security, Phishing and Fraud

Alpine Mobile will never ask a customer to disclose a password or full payment-card PIN through chat.

Be cautious with:
- unexpected suspension links;
- unfamiliar payment websites;
- callers asking for one-time verification codes;
- unknown QR codes.

If fraud is suspected:
1. Do not provide more credentials.
2. Change the affected password.
3. Contact Alpine Mobile through an official channel.
4. Contact the bank or payment provider if financial information may be compromised.

Sensitive account actions require identity verification.
