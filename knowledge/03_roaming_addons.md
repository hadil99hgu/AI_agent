# Roaming Add-ons

## Zone A Data Pack
- 5 GB
- CHF 14.90
- Valid for 30 days from activation

## Zone B Data Pack
- 3 GB
- CHF 24.90
- Valid for 30 days from activation

## Global Data Pack
- 1 GB
- CHF 39.90
- Valid for 30 days from activation
- Available in supported Zone C destinations

An add-on expires when its validity period ends even if data remains unused.
If both an included allowance and an add-on are available, the included allowance is consumed first.
