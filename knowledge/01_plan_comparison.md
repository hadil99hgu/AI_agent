# Mobile Plan Comparison

## Basic
Price: CHF 29.90 per month.
- Unlimited calls and SMS within Switzerland.
- 20 GB of mobile data in Switzerland each month.
- Maximum download speed of 100 Mbps.
- 2 GB of roaming data per month in Zone A.
- Standard customer support.

After 20 GB of Swiss high-speed data, data remains available at reduced speed until the next billing cycle.

## Plus
Price: CHF 49.90 per month.
- Unlimited calls and SMS within Switzerland.
- Unlimited mobile data in Switzerland.
- Maximum download speed of 500 Mbps.
- 20 GB of roaming data per month in Zone A.
- 200 minutes of calls from Switzerland to Zone A countries.
- Multi Device support for one additional device.

## Premium
Price: CHF 69.90 per month.
- Unlimited calls and SMS within Switzerland.
- Unlimited mobile data in Switzerland at the maximum available network speed.
- 40 GB of roaming data per month in Zone A.
- 5 GB of roaming data per month in Zone B.
- Unlimited calls from Switzerland to Zone A countries.
- Multi Device support for up to three additional devices.
- Priority customer support.

Unused monthly roaming allowances do not roll over.
Plan benefits reset on the first day of each billing cycle.
Device financing is billed separately from the mobile subscription.
